Welcome to Sidekick

Use your phone and computer as one: send files both ways, use your phone as a touchpad and keyboard, and connect over Bluetooth when there's no shared Wi-Fi. Everything is encrypted.

This installs Sidekick in your Applications folder. Afterwards, open it from Launchpad or Applications, then install Sidekick on your phone and pair them.

No account, no servers, no analytics. The next page is the privacy policy.
