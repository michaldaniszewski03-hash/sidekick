SIDEKICK PRIVACY POLICY
Effective 2 October 2026 (updated for 2.6.1)

THE SHORT VERSION

Sidekick has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your files go
straight between your own devices, encrypted. Nothing is sent to us.

WHAT SIDEKICK KEEPS ON YOUR DEVICE

- Your settings: the device name, theme, sound, the folder for received
  files, and what paired devices may do.
- Your paired devices: each one's name, device ID, certificate fingerprint,
  and the keys that let the two of you talk securely. These are kept in the
  system's protected storage: the Keychain on iPhone, storage encrypted with
  the Android Keystore on Android, Windows' built-in data protection on
  Windows, and a file only your user account can read on the Mac.
- Its own security identity: a certificate and private key made on the
  device when Sidekick first starts.
- Files you receive, in the folder you choose. On iPhone and Android,
  received photos and videos go to Photos or the gallery instead (on
  Android, in Pictures/Sidekick and Movies/Sidekick).

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Removing Sidekick removes it, except the
files you received, which stay in their folder.

WHAT SIDEKICK SHARES, AND WITH WHOM

Nearby devices. So your devices can find each other, Sidekick announces
itself on your local network and over Bluetooth: its device name, a random
device ID, the kind of device (for example Mac or Android), its Sidekick
version, its network port, the fingerprint of its certificate, and which
features it offers. Anyone on the same network or within Bluetooth range
can see this announcement. It contains no files and no other personal
content.

Paired devices. A device can only do anything with yours after you pair
them with a 6-digit code or a QR code. Paired devices can then:
- ask to send you files (Sidekick asks you to Accept first, unless you turn
  off "Ask before receiving files" in Settings);
- browse your files and use your mouse and keyboard. Both are allowed by
  default; turn either off in Settings under "What paired devices can do
  here". Using the mouse and keyboard also needs the system's Accessibility
  permission on the Mac and Android, which you grant yourself.
You can unpair a device at any time.

Encryption. Everything between paired devices is encrypted: over Wi-Fi with
TLS, checked against each device's own certificate; over Bluetooth with
AES-GCM keys agreed when you paired (SPAKE2, so the code itself never
travels).

PERMISSIONS, AND WHY

- Local network (all devices): to find and talk to your devices.
- Bluetooth (all devices): to connect when there's no shared Wi-Fi.
- Camera (iPhone and Android): only to scan a pairing QR code. The picture
  is read on the device and is never saved or sent.
- Photos (iPhone): only the photos you pick to send, and adding the photos
  and videos you receive to your library. Adding doesn't let Sidekick see
  the rest of your library.
- Files and "All files access" (Android): to save files you receive and,
  if you allow it, let paired devices browse your files.
- Location (Android 12 and older only): those Android versions require it
  for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning. Sidekick never reads or stores your
  location.
- Accessibility (Mac and Android): only so a paired device can move the
  pointer and type, when you allow it.
- Wi-Fi hotspot (Android): for a direct link to your computer when there's
  no shared Wi-Fi. Its name and password go to the paired computer over
  encrypted Bluetooth, and nowhere else.
- Firewall (Windows): the installer lets Sidekick accept connections from
  devices on private networks, and from the local subnet on public networks
  (for the direct link). Uninstalling removes these rules.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reports.
- No servers: Sidekick never contacts us, and it doesn't go online to check
  for updates.
- We don't sell or share data. We don't have any.

THE WEBSITE AND DOWNLOADS

The download page uses no cookies and no analytics. It remembers your light
or dark choice and color in your browser (local storage). It loads its
fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers
(see Google's privacy policy). The downloads are served by GitHub (see
GitHub's privacy statement). If you install Sidekick through another
service, such as AltStore, SideStore, Sideloadly or the Microsoft Store,
that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

CHILDREN

Sidekick doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
Earlier versions are in the project's history on GitHub.
