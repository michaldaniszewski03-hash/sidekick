Features

One app for all of it.

Sidekick does four things: remote control, file browsing, file sharing, and media control. It runs the same way on every device.

touch_app

Remote control, both ways

Turn your phone into a touchpad and keyboard for your PC. From your computer, you can control your Android phone and see its screen.

Move over me · tap to click
folder_open

Browse the other device

Open your phone's storage from your desktop, or your PC's drives from your phone. Select files and pull them over.

send

Send anything, fast

Drag and drop files of any size, share from any app's menu, or sync your clipboard. Nothing goes through the internet.

play_circle

Media controller

Play, pause, seek, skip, and change the volume for whatever is playing on your PC: YouTube, Spotify, VLC, Netflix, and more.

lock

Private by design

You pair devices once with a QR code. After that, every connection is encrypted and stays on your local network. No accounts, no servers.

How it works

Set up in about 30 seconds.

1

Install on both devices

Install Sidekick on your phone and your computer. You don't need an account.

2

Scan the QR code

Point your phone at the code on your computer's screen. The devices remember each other.

3

Start using it

When both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, they connect on their own.
